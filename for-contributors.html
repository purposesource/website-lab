A discussion starter for an issue or a discussion thread
Could we explore Purpose Source for future releases?

It is a published licence (Purpose Source License 1.0) that connects use by larger organisations with funding for public-benefit causes. Contributors keep their copyright, and each release becomes Apache-2.0 after four years.

I’d like us to look at whether it fits our users, our existing licences and our community before making a decision.

What stays yours

No copyright assignment, ever — contributors keep their copyright. You keep contributing through the project’s usual pull requests and reviews.

Recognition is not payment. An Impact Share is an attribution record, not money, ownership or a right to payment. Nothing shows you publicly unless you choose it.
