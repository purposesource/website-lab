How the registry works

What the registry lists, what each row shows, and how a repository gets in.

Rows come from the registry’s published index · Updated 2026-10-28

Every row comes from the registry’s published index, the same data anyone can download as JSON. Nothing else is added to the list.

Who qualifies
A repository under the licence’s unchanged text.
How to apply
No application. Once the public registry is open, a repository under the unchanged text is registered and listed, whether or not anyone claims it.
How it is reviewed
[to be filled from the live page]
The criteria
The unchanged canonical text as the repository’s licence.
The schedule
The public registry opens with the first registered projects; waivers come with the platform’s claim process.

Each row shows the repository’s owner and name, its state, its licence version and its adoption date, with a link to its record. A repository that is suspended, no longer registered or delisted keeps its row and its record.
