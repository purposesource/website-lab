Adoption
Putting the unchanged licence text in a repository’s LICENSE file, for releases you have the right to license.Rule: the adoption guide⁠ (live site)
Apache-2.0 conversion
Each release becomes available under Apache-2.0 four years after it was first public.Rule: licence §7⁠ (live site)
Association
The Purpose Source Association, a Swiss Verein in Aarau and the steward the licence names.Rule: licence §1⁠ (live site)
Certificate
A signed record anyone can verify. A payment certificate attests a settled payment; a status certificate attests a recorded status, such as a waiver.Rule: Certificates
Cost supporter
Someone who settles a listed running-cost invoice in place of the fee account, listed by name and amount (an unconnected private person may ask to stay unnamed).Rule: statutes Art. 6b⁠ (live site)
Cure window
The 60 days to arrange coverage once your use first needs it.Rule: licence §6⁠ (live site)
Entitlement
The annual credential the Association records for an organisation, covering its use within a scope.Rule: licence §2⁠ (live site)
Giveware
Our name for shared software whose licence asks larger organisations for a published fee that goes, after published, capped costs, to listed charities.Rule: How it works
Impact Share
A contributor’s attribution record: recognition, not money.Rule: contributors⁠ (live site)
Large organisation
A group with 100 or more people, or US$1 million or more in revenue. Non-profits, public education and public bodies never count as large for their own use.Rule: licence §5⁠ (live site)
Ledger
The append-only, hash-chained monthly record of fees, costs, the reserve and transfers.Rule: statutes Art. 12⁠ (live site)
The Pass
An Entitlement for every registered repository.Rule: Prices
Project
An Entitlement for one registered repository, at its Standard or Utility price.Rule: Prices
Purpose Condition
The rule that the licence’s permissions apply while you are not using the software for a large organisation, or that organisation holds a current Entitlement or waiver.Rule: licence §4⁠ (live site)
Purpose Fee
The annual fee an organisation pays for an Entitlement.Rule: Prices
PURPOSE.yml
An optional settings file in a repository. Never legal terms.Rule: the settings file⁠ (live site)
Recipient List
The board’s published, versioned list of the organisations that may receive transfers, in seven areas.Rule: statutes Art. 7⁠ (live site)
Recipient Standard
The minimum every recipient meets: the floor in the statutes and the board’s Recipient Rules.Rule: statutes Art. 7⁠ (live site)
Registry
The public record of repositories under the licence, coverage and waivers, which the Association keeps.Rule: statutes Art. 9⁠ (live site)
Transparency log
The append-only log to which every production certificate is added before it is delivered.Rule: Keys and transparency log
Utility repository
Library code under 5,000 lines, at half the Standard price and at most US$1,000.Rule: Prices
Vested part
A part of the software that is yours to keep for good.Rule: licence §2 and §9⁠ (live site)
Waiver
A free, public record by which a repository’s administrators exempt a named organisation. It can never be sold.Rule: licence §9⁠ (live site); statutes Art. 10⁠ (live site)
