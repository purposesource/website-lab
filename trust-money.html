Where the money goes

The pledge, the caps and the path of a fee, each with the article of the statutes that sets it.

Statutes v1, adopted at the founding assembly, October 2026 · No transfer has been made yet · Updated 2026-10-28

The pledge

In its one wording, quoted whole.

100% of net Purpose Fees go to the listed charities within 60 days of each payout, after published, capped costs: running costs at most 15% of a year's net fees, and the reserve at most 5% of each payout until it holds six months of costs, so at least 80% every year. Listed supporters lower the costs, never what is passed on. No cap is ever raised for a purchase already made. Every cost and transfer is published monthly.

The path of a fee

  1. 1

    An organisation pays

    Through Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record.

  2. 2

    Net Purpose Fees

    The payment provider’s fee and the taxes charged at purchase come off, and so do refunds and chargebacks. What remains are the net Purpose Fees. Set by Art. 5⁠ (live site)

  3. 3

    Running costs

    At most 15% of a financial year’s net Purpose Fees, people included. Every invoice is published monthly; people as one line per function, never by name. Set by Art. 6, 6f⁠ (statutes on the live site)

  4. 4

    Reserve

    At most 5% of each payout, until the reserve holds six months of costs. It starts at zero. Set by Art. 6g⁠ (live site)

  5. 5

    Listed charities

    The rest, within 60 days of each payout, directly from the fee account: one transfer per listed recipient. Set by Art. 6a⁠ (live site)

  6. 6

    Publication

    Every cost and transfer, monthly, each line to its evidence. Set by Art. 6e⁠ (live site)

What never happens

  • No cap is ever raised for a purchase already made (Art. 22⁠ (live site) para. 4).
  • Purpose Fee money never funds another activity of the Association (Art. 6c⁠ (live site)).
  • No distributable private profit — structurally, not by policy (Art. 5⁠ (live site)).

Running-cost supporters

Anyone may settle a listed running-cost invoice in place of the fee account. Each supporter is listed for the month, by name and amount; a private person with no tie to a buyer, recipient or registered project may ask to stay unnamed. Support never exceeds the month’s running costs and never passes through the fee account.

Held by: statutes Art. 6b⁠ (live site)
