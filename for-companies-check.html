Do I need to pay?

Four questions from sections 4 and 5 of the licence. This page stores nothing and sends nothing; the licence text governs.

Each answer is a link · works without JavaScript · Updated 2026-10-28

Four questions

1. Are you using the software only for yourself, not for an organisation’s work?

  • Yes
  • No

2. Is the organisation a non-profit or public-benefit body barred from distributing profit, a public educational institution, or a public body, using it for its own work?

  • Yes
  • No

A commercial organisation that such a body controls is measured like any other.

3. Is the use only non-production evaluation, a security review, or preparing contributions to the project?

  • Yes
  • No

4. Across the whole group, in each member’s latest completed tax year: fewer than 100 people (employees and individual contractors) and less than US$1 million revenue?

  • Yes
  • No, or not sure

Until reliable figures exist, the licence lets you use reasonable provisional ones.

The answers

Free: personal use

No fee and no registration. Personal use, unrelated to work for an organisation, needs no coverage (licence §4).

Start again

Free: non-profit, public education or public body

No coverage is needed for its own use, whatever its size (licence §5).

Start again

Free: evaluation, review or contributing

Allowed for everyone without a credential. Production use, or offering the software’s functionality to others, is not included (licence §4).

Start again

Free: below the threshold

No fee and no registration while both stay true. You assess your own size; nobody may audit you (licence §5).

Start again

Coverage needed

An Entitlement (Project or the Pass), or a waiver from the project’s administrators. You have 60 days to arrange it (licence §6). Work done solely for a client is covered by that client’s coverage (licence §4).
