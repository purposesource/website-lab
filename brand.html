Name, logos and badges

What anyone may show and say about Purpose Source, and what needs our agreement.

Permission is free and non-exclusive · no prior approval of your wording

Allowed without asking

  • Mention our name and link to our website.
  • Use the official logos and badges in READMEs, presentations, announcements and posts.
  • Describe your participation in your own words, in any language. No approval is needed.

Needs our agreement first

  • Anything that suggests an endorsement, a partnership or authority to speak for the Association.

Never allowed

  • Distorting, redrawing or combining the artwork into a different mark.
  • Using the marks in deceptive, unlawful, hateful or abusive material.
