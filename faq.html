Everyone

Is this open source?

No. The Purpose Source License is source-available: public code, collaboration and forking, with a coverage condition for use by larger organisations. It is not OSI-approved. Each release becomes Apache-2.0 four years after its publication.

Rests on: licence §4 and §7⁠ (live site); Compare licensing models

Why not open source with donations? Draft

Donations are optional and hard to plan on. Purpose Source makes a published fee part of the licence for larger organisations, and the statutes cap what the Association may keep before the rest goes to listed charities.

Rests on: Where the money goes

Why not BSL, FSL or PolyForm? Draft

BSL and FSL let one vendor delay a permissive release of its own product, and PolyForm offers standard licences that each project applies alone. Purpose Source is one shared licence with one steward: an organisation can cover every registered project with one Pass, and the fees go, after published, capped costs, to listed charities rather than to a vendor.

PolyForm Small Business uses the same test of 100 people and US$1 million; its figure is indexed to inflation, ours is not.

Rests on: Compare licensing models

Who owns the code?

The people who wrote it. Contributors keep their copyright, and the Association never becomes the owner or licensor of code it registers for others.

Rests on: statutes Art. 4⁠ (live site); licence §2–3⁠ (live site)

Can my project still earn badges that require a FLOSS licence? Draft

Not for releases under this licence. The OpenSSF Best Practices badge, for example, requires a FLOSS licence. Each release becomes Apache-2.0 four years after its publication.

Rests on: licence §7⁠ (live site)

Project owners

What does adoption take?

Your rights cleared, then one file: the unchanged licence text as your LICENSE, for releases you have the right to license. No account, application, signature or ongoing report is needed to use the licence.

Rests on: Adopt in five steps; the adoption guide⁠ (live site)

Can I leave?

Yes. Where you hold the rights, you can choose another licence for future releases. There is no exit fee, minimum term or notification form. Earlier releases keep their terms, including vested coverage and each release’s four-year conversion.

Rests on: Leaving the licence⁠ (live site)

Can I exempt a company from the fee?

Yes, with a waiver. The administrators of a claimed repository can excuse a named organisation for that repository, free of charge and always in public. A waiver can never be sold.

Rests on: licence §9⁠ (live site); statutes Art. 10⁠ (live site)

Contributors

Am I paid?

No. Purpose Source does not pay contributors, and the statutes bar paying anyone for a contribution out of Purpose Fees. You keep your copyright and may receive recognition: an Impact Share, which is an attribution record and not money or ownership, and an advisory choice of causes, which starts in a trial period.

Rests on: statutes Art. 5⁠ (live site) para. 2

Companies

Who needs to pay?

An organisation whose whole group had 100 or more people (employees and individual contractors) or US$1,000,000 or more revenue, in each member’s latest completed tax year. Below both, use is free.

Non-profit and public-benefit bodies, public educational institutions and public bodies never need coverage for their own use; a commercial organisation that such a body controls is measured like any other. You assess your own size, and nobody may audit you. The revenue figure is not indexed to inflation.

Rests on: licence §5⁠ (live site); Do I need to pay?

What is the 60-day cure window?

The licence allows 60 days to arrange coverage once your use first needs it, and at most 60 such days in any twelve months. Parts that have already vested stay covered.

Rests on: licence §6⁠ (live site)

Can we donate directly instead?

No. There is no direct-donation route. Coverage comes from an Entitlement under the published price list, or from a waiver that a repository’s administrators grant.

Rests on: Prices

Will our licence scanner recognise it?

Use LicenseRef-PurposeSource-1.0. The licence has no SPDX listing; a request of 2026-10-01 was withdrawn. The procurement and legal pack explains custom identifiers.

Rests on: Files and identifiers; the procurement and legal pack⁠ (live site)

Will anyone audit us?

No. You assess your own size, and nobody may audit you under the licence.

Rests on: licence §5 and §10⁠ (live site)

If we stop paying, what happens to what we use?

Every part within your credential’s scope that was public by the end of your term stays covered permanently. Each release also becomes Apache-2.0 after four years.

Rests on: licence §9⁠ (live site)

The Association and money

Where does the money go?

100% of net Purpose Fees go to the listed charities within 60 days of each payout, after published, capped costs: running costs at most 15% of a year's net fees, and the reserve at most 5% of each payout until it holds six months of costs, so at least 80% every year. Listed supporters lower the costs, never what is passed on. No cap is ever raised for a purchase already made. Every cost and transfer is published monthly.

Rests on: Where the money goes; statutes Art. 5, 6, 6a, 6e, 6g⁠ (statutes on the live site)

Who pays the running costs?

Net Purpose Fees, within the 15% cap. Listed supporters can settle listed invoices instead, which lowers the costs and never what is passed on.

Rests on: statutes Art. 6, 6b⁠ (statutes on the live site)

Can a company buy influence?

No. Paying buys coverage and an optional listing, nothing else.

Rests on: What money cannot buy
