How to read a row

Registered
Under the licence’s unchanged text and listed, with its own page and badge, whether or not anyone claims it.
Suspended, delisted
The badge shows a neutral status. The row and its record stay, so the history stays readable.
No longer registered
The project chose another licence for future releases. Earlier releases keep their terms.

A listing is not an endorsement.
