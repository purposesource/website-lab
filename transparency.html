How the ledger works

  1. Append-only: a row is never edited or deleted; a correction is a new row (Art. 12⁠ (live site)).
  2. Hash-chained: each row’s hash covers the row before it, so changing a row breaks every later hash.
  3. Locked before transfer: the payouts credited in a month are allocated and locked together, then transferred.
  4. Exact: amounts are kept in whole minor units and never rounded.
  5. Four kinds of outgoing line only: transfers to listed recipients, listed third-party invoices, personnel costs and the reserve retention (Art. 6a⁠ (live site)).
  6. One page and one CSV file per month.
