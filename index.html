The Purpose Source License is source-available. People and small organisations use the software free. Larger organisations pay a published annual fee; after the payment provider’s fee and published, capped costs, the fees go to listed charities. A project adopts it by changing one file.

How it works

  1. 1

    A project adopts the licence

    One file, once your rights are cleared. The dashboard can open the pull request for you, under your own GitHub account.

    Adopt in five steps⁠
  2. 2

    Larger organisations take coverage

    An annual Entitlement, for one project or for every registered repository (the Pass), priced by revenue band and sold by Paddle, our reseller.

    Prices⁠
  3. 3

    The fees pass on

    After published, capped costs, to listed charities in seven areas. Every cost and transfer is published monthly.

    The seven areas: health, education, poverty relief, humanitarian aid, the environment, animal welfare and research.

Check it yourself

Five records anyone can open.

  • Certificates you verify in your browser
  • The monthly ledger
  • The statutes that bind the money⁠ (live site)
