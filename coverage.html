How coverage is decided

One question, eight fixed answers, and only published documents as inputs.

Answer set frozen · switched on in production at launch

“Is this organisation covered for this repository?” has one frozen set of eight answers, computed by a versioned function over published documents only: the organisation’s signed Entitlement record, the repository’s record and the public list of waivers.

Until launch, coverage is proved by the signed Entitlement record, which the verification page checks.
