The board

Two members at founding, named by role: the president and the treasurer. The board has two to seven members, elected by the general assembly for two years; re-election is permitted.

The board keeps published which of its members are paid, and who holds an interest in a registered project, a recipient, a payer or a processor. The interests register is published with the annual transparency report.

Names appear in the legal notice once the register entry is published.

Held by: statutes Art. 18⁠ (live site)

Waivers
Who qualifies
The administrators of a claimed repository grant them; any named organisation can receive one.
How to apply
Ask the repository’s administrators. [to be filled]
How it is reviewed
A verified administrator grants it after re-authenticating; every co-administrator is notified.
The criteria
Free, public, one repository; never sold.
The schedule
Covered at once; permanent rights attach after a 72-hour cooling window.
Joining the Recipient List
Who qualifies
A not-for-profit organisation with a charitable or public-benefit purpose in one of the seven areas.
How to apply
An organisation may propose itself; there is no open self-registration.
How it is reviewed
Every addition is a minuted board decision, never made on a payer’s or a contributor’s nomination alone.
The criteria
The floor in the statutes (Art. 7) and the board’s Recipient Rules.
The schedule
An addition takes effect after thirty days’ public notice.
Advisory votes
Who qualifies
The people the board asks. Staff and board members do not answer.
How to apply
No application: people answer in the Voting tab of their dashboard.
How it is reviewed
The board publishes the result and its own answer next to it.
The criteria
The answers are advice; the board decides.
The schedule
Answers can be changed or withdrawn until the vote closes.
Membership
Who qualifies
Natural persons who contribute in a sustained way. Never acquired by paying a Purpose Fee, buying a credential, administering a repository or contributing; companies cannot be members.
How to apply
A written application.
How it is reviewed
A resolution of the general assembly.
The criteria
Sustained contribution (statutes Art. 15 and 16).
