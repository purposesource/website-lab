Adopt the licence

Adoption is one file, once your rights are cleared: the unchanged licence text as your LICENSE. You keep your repository, your decisions and your contributors’ copyright. The dashboard can open the pull request for you, under your own GitHub account.

Free · no account or separate agreement needed to adopt · Updated 2026-10-28

Is it right for your project?

  • Fees from larger users go, after published, capped costs, to listed charities. The model fits teams who want the use of their software to fund public benefit.
  • You must be able to license the work, and be comfortable asking larger users to take coverage.
  • Some employers and distribution channels accept only OSI-approved licences. Check their policies first: Purpose Source is source-available.

What stays yours

  • Repository administration, what you merge and what you release.
  • Your contributors’ copyright: no copyright assignment, ever — contributors keep their copyright.
  • The right to choose another licence for future releases, where you hold the rights. Earlier releases keep their terms.
