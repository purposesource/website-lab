For companies

You pay only if your group has 100 or more people or US$1 million or more in revenue; non-profits, public education and public bodies never pay for their own use. Coverage for one repository costs US$150 to US$4,500 a year, and for every registered repository (the Pass) US$1,500 to US$22,500, by revenue band, before tax.

What coverage is

An Entitlement records your organisation’s permission to use the software within a chosen scope and an annual term.

The source stays in the project’s repository, and its developers maintain and license the code. The Association issues and records the coverage. Paddle, our merchant of record, handles payment, invoices and sales tax.

Project or the Pass

Project
One registered repository you choose, at its Standard or Utility price. Utility means library code under 5,000 lines: half the Standard price, at most US$1,000.
The Pass
Every registered repository, for one annual fee.
How the Pass shares your fee

You may name up to fifty registered repositories you use; each is attributed 1% of your passed-on share, and the rest is split equally across all registered repositories active at each month’s lock.

After you buy

Every part within your scope that is public by the end of your term stays covered permanently. A part counts as public from the day anyone can first get it, for example from a public commit.

The term renews each year unless you turn renewal off. Each release also becomes Apache-2.0 four years after its publication.
