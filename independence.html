What money cannot buy

Paying buys licence coverage. It buys nothing else.

Version 1 · Draft wording, not yet approved · Updated 2026-10-28

1.Paying buys coverage, and an optional listing

An Entitlement covers your use within its scope and term. Your organisation’s name appears only if you ask.

Held by: Entitlement terms⁠ (live site); What you buy: public listing⁠ (live site)

2.Money gives no say

Paying a Purpose Fee or buying a credential never makes you a member, and gives no governance rights, roadmap priority or listing prominence. A payer’s choice of causes is advisory, like everyone’s.

Held by: statutes Art. 15, 7, 8⁠ (statutes on the live site); Entitlement terms⁠ (live site)

3.One price list for everyone

No private prices, for anyone.

Held by: statutes Art. 11⁠ (live site)

4.Waivers are never sold

A waiver is free and public. Selling or brokering one is forbidden.

Held by: statutes Art. 10⁠ (live site); licence §9⁠ (live site)

5.Support for running costs is published

Every supporter is listed for the month, by name and amount; only a private person with no tie to a buyer, recipient or registered project may ask to stay unnamed. Support never exceeds the month’s running costs and never passes through the fee account.

Held by: statutes Art. 6b⁠ (live site)

6.A listing is not an endorsement

Being named as a covered organisation or as a supporter says nothing about your products.

Held by: [proposed wording; no article yet]
