Photographs

Each photograph is under a free licence: CC0, which asks nothing, or CC BY 4.0, which asks us to name the author and the licence and to say what we changed. We crop and resize every photograph and remove its metadata; anything more is listed with it. The people shown do not speak for Purpose Source.

Why this page

On the real site: none yet · new page, for the photographs

Study recommendations this page shows

  • 11Records you can check: Verify, provenance, a report address: each photograph’s provenance: its source page, author, licence and what we changed

Design direction of 2026-10-29

  • 1More room: sections 72 px apart, wider margins, a reading column of about 70 characters
  • 2Two warm type families: Source Serif 4 for headings and text, Inter for menus, labels, metadata and tables; no third font
  • 3Humanised images: the six licensed photographs, each with its author, source, licence and changes; linked from every footer as “Photo credits”
  • 5A softer palette: warm grey text, a softer green, sage tints; dark mode on deep slate green
  • 6Gentle motion: pages cross-fade, sections rise into view as you scroll, menus and accordions open softly; all of it off with reduced motion

Left out on purpose

  • Photographs of the Association’s own people: it names roles, not people, until the register entry.
