About the Association

The Purpose Source Association is a Swiss association (Verein) with its seat in Aarau, founded in October 2026. It publishes the Purpose Source License, keeps the public registry, issues coverage to larger organisations and passes the fees, after published, capped costs, to listed charities.

Commercial-register entry pending publication · Altstadtbüro, Zollrain 2, 5000 Aarau

What the Association does

  • Publishes the licence: one shared text, versioned and hashed.
  • Keeps the registry: the public record of repositories under the licence, coverage and waivers.
  • Issues coverage: annual Entitlements for larger organisations, sold by Paddle, our reseller.
  • Passes the fees on: after the payment provider’s fee and published, capped costs, to listed charities.
  • Publishes the records: every cost and transfer, monthly; decisions and votes; the documents behind them.

The Association never owns or licenses code it registers for others (statutes Art. 4⁠ (live site)).

Funding

Larger organisations pay Purpose Fees. After the payment provider’s fee and published, capped running costs and reserve, the fees go to listed charities. People and small organisations pay nothing.

The Association’s other activities keep separate accounts, and Purpose Fee money never funds them.

Who runs it

A board of two members at founding: the president and the treasurer. This site names people by role. The representative under Art. 69 Abs. 2 ZGB is named in the legal notice once the register entry is published.
