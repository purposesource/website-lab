Compare licensing models

Purpose Source is source-available, not OSI-approved. It keeps public code, collaboration and forking, with a published fee for larger organisations.

Updated 2026-10-28

The short version

  • Public code, collaboration in the repository and forking stay as people know them.
  • Larger organisations need coverage: an Entitlement, a waiver or another recorded permission.
  • Buying coverage funds public-benefit recipients; it does not pay the project’s maintainers.
  • Each release becomes Apache-2.0 four years after its publication.

Questions people ask

  • Why not open source with donations?
  • Why not BSL, FSL or PolyForm?
  • Can my project still earn badges that require a FLOSS licence?
  • Who needs to pay?
